Privacy policy

How Space Battle X handles your data: what stays on the device, what goes through Google Play and Google Play Games, and when the game uses the internet.

Android app: com.efsaplicativos.spacebattlex
Last updated: November 25, 2026.

Space Battle X shows no ads, asks for no sign-up, uses no usage analytics or tracking tools and has no server of its own: the developer receives no player data. The game works offline; the internet is only used for the Full game purchase and for Google Play Games achievements and leaderboards.

1. What stays on your device

Campaign progress, cleared stages, credits and ship upgrades, unlocked ships, records, statistics, achievements and settings are stored only on the device, in the game's own file and an automatic copy of it. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include that file in the device backup, as it does for other apps. This is controlled in Android settings, not in the game.

2. Full game purchase

The first 6 stages are free. The Full game, which unlocks the other stages, is a one-time purchase made on Google Play's own screen: the game never sees your payment details. Google only tells the game whether the purchase was made, so the stages unlock, also when you reinstall the game or switch devices with the same Google account.

The game talks to Google Play through Unity's purchasing library (Unity In-App Purchasing), which is built into the game. According to Unity, this library collects and sends to Unity, encrypted, data needed to make purchases work and to measure its performance: approximate location (from the IP address), device and installation identifiers, the history of purchases made through the library, crash logs, and diagnostic and performance data. This data is not sold or used for ads. What Unity does with it follows Unity's privacy policy, and what Google does with purchase data follows Google's privacy policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

3. Achievements and leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you use Google Play Games on the device, the game signs in to your Play Games account when it opens. You can also sign in, or play without an account, from the button on the Leaderboards screen. Without an account the game works the same, with records and achievements kept only on the device.

With the account connected, the game sends Google:

  • the achievements you unlock;
  • your scores, for the online leaderboards;
  • the Play Games player ID, which Google itself provides to identify the account in this game.

The game does not receive your email, name or password. On leaderboards, other players see the name and avatar of your Play Games profile, according to your profile's visibility settings. To delete this game's Play Games data, use the Google Play Games settings on the device. What Google does with this data follows Google's privacy policy.

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for the purchase and for Google Play Games.
  • Google Play billing: for the Full game purchase.
  • Vibration: for in-game vibration, which can be adjusted or turned off in the options.
  • Notifications (Android 13 or newer): declared by a Unity library included in the game. The game does not request this permission and shows no notifications.

6. Children

Space Battle X is not directed at children under 13. The game asks no one for age, name or other personal data; the only data that leaves the device is what the sections above describe.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the game update, with the new date at the top.

8. Contact

EFS Aplicativos is responsible for Space Battle X. Privacy questions can be sent to efsaplicativos@hotmail.com.
