Privacy policy

How Lance Certo handles your data: what stays on your device, what the ads collect and when the app uses the internet.

Android app: com.efsAplicativos.lancecerto
Last updated: November 2, 2026.

Lance Certo is a chess training app made to work offline. It shows ads from Google AdMob, described in the Ads section. Apart from that, it uses no analytics or tracking tools, asks for no sign-up and sends no data to its developer: the app has no server of its own.

1. What stays on your device

Everything you do in the app is kept only on your device: puzzle attempts and rating, repertoires and reviews, finished lessons, imported games and their analysis, XP, streak, daily goal and settings. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

If Android backup is turned on in your Google account, Android may include this data in the device backup, as it does for other apps. This is controlled in Android's settings, not in the app.

2. Backup to a file

On the progress screen, you can save a backup of your data to a file. You choose where it goes: on the device or in a service such as Google Drive, through Android's file picker. The file holds everything the app keeps about you, including the Lichess and Chess.com usernames you typed, but not the token of your Lichess account. The app never sends the file anywhere; it only reads a backup when you pick the file to restore.

3. Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the top and another at the bottom of the screens without a board, such as the menus, an ad when the app opens and, at most once every three minutes, a full-screen ad when you open a study, such as a lesson, a training session, puzzles, a game or an opening. Without internet, no ad is requested. If you buy Remove ads, you see none of them.

To choose and show the ads, measure their results and prevent fraud, Google's ads SDK, which ships inside the app, collects and sends to Google, encrypted:

  • the device's IP address, which gives its approximate location;
  • the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, if any, identifiers of the accounts signed in on the device;
  • how you use the app and the ads: when the app opens and which ads were seen and tapped;
  • diagnostic data, such as the app's launch time, hangs and battery use.

What Google does with this data follows its own policy, explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where the law requires it, as in the European Union and the United Kingdom, the app first shows Google's consent message, and you choose whether to accept personalized ads. In those places, the About screen has the Ad privacy options button, to change that choice at any time. Anywhere, you can delete or reset the advertising ID in Android's settings.

4. Remove ads purchase

The app offers a one-time purchase, Remove ads, that removes all the ads. You pay on Google Play's own screen, and the app never sees your payment details: Google only tells the app whether the purchase was made, so that the ads go away, also on a new device with the same Google account. For buyers, Google's ads SDK is not even started. What Google does with the purchase data follows its own policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

5. When the app uses the internet

The app only connects for the features below, and each request goes straight from the device to the service:

As with any connection, these services also receive the device's IP address, and in the requests to Lichess and Chess.com the app identifies itself as "Lance Certo Android app", as their APIs ask. What each service does with this follows its own policy: Lichess and Chess.com.

6. Lichess account (optional)

The Lichess opening explorer only answers people signed in with a Lichess account, so the app offers that sign-in on the repertoire screen. It is optional: the rest of the app works without an account.

  • You sign in on Lichess's own page, in the browser. The app never sees your password.
  • The app asks for no special permission on your account: the access token Lichess hands over only reads public data, such as the explorer.
  • The token is stored on the device, encrypted with an Android key that never leaves the device, and is left out of backups (Android's and the app's backup file).
  • The token is only sent to Lichess: to learn your account's name when you sign in, in the explorer's requests and to cancel it when you sign out. With it, Lichess knows those requests come from your account.
  • When you sign out in the app, the token is deleted from the device and the app asks Lichess to cancel it. You can also revoke it at any time in your Lichess account's security settings.

7. Notifications

The daily reminder is optional and stays off until you turn it on on the progress screen. It is scheduled and shown by the device itself, without going through any server, and only appears on days when the XP goal hasn't been met yet.

8. Permissions

  • Internet: for the features in the table above.
  • Advertising ID and network state: declared by Google's ads SDK, to read the advertising ID and to know whether there is a connection before requesting an ad.
  • Google Play billing: declared by Google's billing library, for the Remove ads purchase.
  • Notifications (Android 13 or newer): asked for only when you turn on the daily reminder.
  • Android's task scheduler (WorkManager), which the app uses for the reminder, declares the permissions it needs to keep the reminder scheduled after the device restarts.

9. Children

Lance Certo is not directed at children under 13. The app asks no one for their age, name or other personal data; what leaves the device is only what the sections above describe.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the app update, with the new date at the top.
